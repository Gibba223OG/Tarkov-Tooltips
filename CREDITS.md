## Changes in Tarkov Tooltips

This fork/derivative adds and modifies features including:
- Separate native Tauri/WebView2 popups for price/quest information and ammunition information.
- Wiki-derived ammunition statistics and armor-class indicators.
- Independent popup scaling, adjustable popup colors, and optional bold price styling.
- Price, Quest and Ammo desktop test buttons.
- Revised settings interface, screen-recognition and calibration workflows.

These changes do not erase or replace the original author's contribution.
